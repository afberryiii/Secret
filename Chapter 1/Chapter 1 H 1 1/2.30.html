You crouch down in front of Ben, grabbing his shoulders to force him to look at you, your urgency absolute. "Forget Alex. He's a dead end right now. Tell me about the journal. What exactly did you see?" Ben's breathing is ragged. He finally collapses against the lockers, defeated. "Okay, okay, just keep your voice down! We were in the host's garage, looking for more beer, and we found this old wooden box hidden under a tarp. Alex opened it—it had a lock, but it was busted. Inside, there was this journal, an old one, and a picture. The journal was filled with creepy stuff... names, dates, and these detailed revenge fantasies against people who apparently 'wronged' the person who wrote it." You press him, your voice urgent. "Whose names? Did you recognize anyone?" "Yeah, that's the messed up part. The very last entry, written in different ink, was about how the writer was going to 'make them all pay' for a secret from years ago. And the picture... it was a faded photo of a high school group, all dressed up—maybe for prom, years ago. But the people in the picture... they were our parents," Ben finally manages, his eyes wide with horror. "Alex freaked out. He snatched the journal and the picture, shoved them in his backpack, and told me that if I ever breathed a word of it, he'd blame everything on me. He said we had to keep it secret because it was about our families." He looks up the hallway again, his paranoia peaking. "I don't know the names on the revenge list, I only saw initials! I swear, I didn't mean for anything bad to happen. Alex has the journal in his backpack right now. He swore he'd burn it after school!" The revelation lands like a ton of bricks. The killer isn't just a random threat; they're targeting your friend group based on an old secret involving your parents. The journal contains the names of the original targets and perhaps the identity of the person seeking revenge. If Alex destroys it, your only lead is gone.
