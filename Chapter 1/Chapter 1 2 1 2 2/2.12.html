Chloe is still staring intensely at her test, her posture rigid, her pencil barely moving. The tension around her is a tangible thing, but she doesn't once touch her phone, which sits face-down beside her desk. David, the tech wizard, is also quiet, but he occasionally slides his phone slightly out from under his desk, his thumb hovering over the screen as if waiting for a notification, before quickly tucking it away. This confirms your instinct that he's the one you should talk to next; he's clearly looking for data. You then turn your attention to the rest of the group. The most unusual behavior comes from Ben. You notice his phone is propped up against his textbook, and though the screen is dark, a subtle flicker of light catches your eye every few minutes—like a message is being received, only for the screen to dim again before he can react. He never fully checks it, but he seems agonizingly aware of its presence. The bell rings, shattering the silence. You've gathered no proof, but you've narrowed your focus: Chloe is hiding something, David is ready to dig, and Ben is highly anxious and possibly receiving secret communications.
