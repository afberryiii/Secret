You decide to test the waters with someone else in your social circle, opting for a softer approach than confronting Chloe again. You catch Sarah as she's packing up her books after the Chem test, an easy target since she's the most perceptive and least likely to instantly dismiss you. "Hey, Sarah, can I ask you something weird?" you begin, trying to keep your tone light. "Only if it's about how totally unfair that surprise stoichiometry question was," she replies, rolling her eyes as she slings her backpack over one shoulder. "Worse than stoichiometry. I had this super vivid nightmare last night," you continue, leaning in conspiratorially. "I dreamed Chloe and I were being chased by a killer after prom, down our street. Ghostface, the whole nine yards." Sarah stops zipping her bag. Her expression doesn't shift much, but the slight narrowing of her eyes tells you she's paying attention. "Oh, gross. I hate those. They're always too real," she says, a genuine shiver running through her. "Did you look it up? Sometimes those dreams are about feeling stressed or anxious about something in real life, like college apps or something." You shake your head. "Maybe, but... Chloe was acting really weird when I brought it up. Like she was trying to shut it down fast. Did she say anything to you about the walk home? She said it was the best night ever, but... I don't know." Sarah taps a finger on her chin thoughtfully. "No, she just said you guys walked, talked, and got home. Nothing dramatic. Look, maybe she just thinks it's a ridiculous topic to dwell on," Sarah offers, though her voice lacks conviction. She glances down the hall toward the main stairwell, then leans in closer one last time. "But you know Chloe. She can be a little dramatic and private when she wants to be. If something happened, I'm sure it'd come out eventually. Don't sweat it." Her advice is sound, but the seed of doubt about Chloe remains. Sarah's slight hesitation only fuels your suspicion.
