You meet Sarah behind the bleachers just outside the back gate. She's already started Kevin's beat-up pickup truck, the engine idling roughly. She opens the passenger door for you, her face grim. "Did you bring anything useful?" she asks as you climb in. You pat your pocket where you've stashed a heavy, rusty wrench you found near the gym—hardly a professional tool, but better than nothing. "This is the best I could do. Did he move?" Sarah shakes her head, pulling out onto the road with a screech of tires. "The tracker is dead-on. He's at the Reel Life. Look, I'm scared. But I know Alex. He's furious, and he's desperate to protect his family's reputation. If that journal has the truth, he will burn it." The drive is tense and silent. When you pull up two blocks from the abandoned video store, you kill the engine and proceed on foot. The building is a skeletal shadow, its glass windows opaque with dust and years of grime. The back lot is dark and overgrown. "You take the back door. I'll cover the front windows and call if I see anyone, or if I hear a scream," Sarah whispers, her voice tight but resolved. "Don't go in alone. Wait for me." But as you creep up to the back door, you hear a frantic, muffled sound from inside—a sound of something heavy slamming against metal, followed by a muffled, sharp cry. The evidence is inside, and it sounds like Alex is in trouble.
